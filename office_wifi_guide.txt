# Company Office Wi-Fi and Guest Access Guide

## 1. Corporate Wi-Fi (For Employees)
- Network Name (SSID): IntelliDesk-Staff
- Security: WPA2-Enterprise
- Username: Your corporate email address (e.g., john@intellidesk.ai)
- Password: Your current corporate password
- Recommended Frequency: 5 GHz band for best speed and latency.

## 2. Guest Wi-Fi (For Visitors & Clients)
- Network Name (SSID): IntelliDesk-Guest
- Password: Guest@Desk2026
- Speed Limit: 25 Mbps per connected device.
- Note: The guest network provides internet access only and cannot reach internal servers, GitLab, or company printers.

## 3. Wi-Fi Troubleshooting Steps
If you cannot connect to IntelliDesk-Staff:
1. Turn off Wi-Fi on your laptop, wait 5 seconds, and turn it back on.
2. Right-click the network name and choose "Forget Network".
3. Re-select "IntelliDesk-Staff" and type your corporate email and password again.
4. If your password was recently changed, update your stored Wi-Fi credentials in Windows Network Settings.
